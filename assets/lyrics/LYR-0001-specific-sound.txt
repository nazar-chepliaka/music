It may have been a little weird,
but I don't need to show you the world.
I have an aim to comprehend
that my mind is so embedded
in that strange and independent
specific sound that I mentioned
a hundred songs before my grand
entry to this game — and Bentley
will definitely wait for me in the same place.

This dummy race
looks like a chase.
Just check my trace —
I always keep one step ahead of you,
my little friend, try to assume
who will leave the bad room
without a résumé.

But hey,
I have already made that mistake,
so I hope that I
will put on the brakes.
The main task for me
is not to be fake,
so I want to bake my personal cake.

Daybreak will mark a new achievement,
and I will start a new assignment
that will be done before the evening,
while everybody else is dreaming.
